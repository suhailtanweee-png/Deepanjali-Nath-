05 / KNOWLEDGE BASE

Cybersecurity Articles

01 — What is ethical cybersecurity?

Ethical security work focuses on identifying and reducing risks with explicit permission, a defined scope and responsible reporting. Never test other people's accounts or systems without authorisation.

02 — How to protect your Instagram account

Use a unique password, turn on two-factor authentication, protect your recovery email, review login activity and avoid third-party apps that ask for your credentials.

03 — How to recognise phishing

Be cautious with urgent threats, unexpected attachments, unfamiliar login links and requests for passwords or one-time codes. Visit the service directly instead of using a message link.

04 — AI and responsible security

AI can help explain concepts and organise information, but it can also make mistakes. Check important claims against trustworthy documentation and avoid submitting private data to tools without understanding their policies.

05 — Beginner's web security checklist

Keep software updated, use HTTPS, validate input, protect secrets, apply least privilege and test only environments you own or are authorised to assess.
